Data Deletion Request

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data associated with PlayMENA games. It currently applies to Hexa Puzzle: Color Sort, Arrow GO! Challenge, and Blast Loop, and will cover future PlayMENA games as they are released.

1. What Data We Hold

Our games can be played anonymously, without creating an account. You may optionally sign in with Google, where a game offers it, to save and sync your progress (Apple sign-in may be added later). Depending on how you play, we may hold:

  • Game progress & player profile: stored on our game backend, Microsoft PlayFab, on servers operated by Microsoft — not only on your device. Even when you play anonymously, a PlayFab player record is created and identified by an anonymous device identifier.
  • Advertising & analytics data: your device advertising ID and usage/crash data collected through Google AdMob and Google Firebase / Google Analytics. In games that include it, app activity and your advertising ID, where it is available, also go to Meta through Facebook App Events, whether or not you have a Facebook account. On Android, the advertising ID is collected unless you have removed it in your device settings. On iOS, it is available only where you allow tracking.
  • Push notification token: a token that identifies your device for push notifications, collected through Google Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Connected-account data (only if you sign in): when you sign in with Google, your PlayFab progress is linked to your Google account so you can play across multiple devices and restore your data after reinstalling. We receive basic profile information from Google (such as your name and Google account identifier).

2. Delete It Yourself

You can manage some data on your own at any time:

  • Manage Facebook App Events: Meta holds the data it receives through Facebook App Events under its own privacy policy. If you have a Facebook account, you can review and disconnect this activity in your activity off Meta technologies.
  • Reset your advertising ID: in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) to limit ad tracking.

Important: because your progress is stored on our servers (PlayFab), uninstalling the game does not delete it. To remove your server-side data, send us a deletion request as described below.

3. Request a Full Deletion

To request deletion of any data we hold about you — including progress linked to a connected account and analytics data — email us at info@playmena.io with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Please include:

  • The game name (e.g. Hexa Puzzle);
  • Whether you played anonymously or signed in with an account;
  • If you signed in, the name on that account (and, if possible, send the request from the email linked to that account);
  • If you played anonymously, your Player ID if the game shows one (usually in the game's settings or "about" screen), so we can find the right record.

We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

4. What We Delete & Retain

Upon a verified request, we delete your PlayFab player record (your game progress and profile), unlink any connected account, and disassociate analytics data tied to your identifiers. We may retain limited information where required by law, for fraud prevention, or in routine backups, which are overwritten on a rolling basis. Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be retained.

5. Contact

Questions about deleting your data? Email info@playmena.io.
